## IncogBoard (current name, checked 2026-11-24)

Not legal advice. A first screen by web search; the official registers (USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO, IP India, UKIPO) were
not queried directly, so their status must be confirmed on the official sites or by a professional search.

- **Exact name:** a web search for "IncogBoard" found no product, app, company or register entry with that name.
- **Close names found:** Incogneato (anonymous feedback service, formerly Corpell Anonymous Box), IncogNote
  (an "incognito notepad" Android app), "Incog: Campus Rant & Real Talk" (a social app on Google Play) and
  "Incognito Away - Disable Incog" (a browser-incognito blocker). None is a keyboard or input method. No register
  record for "INCOG" or "INCOGBOARD" showed up in the snippets.
- **Descriptive-word risk:** "incognito" is a common word that keyboards (SwiftKey, Gboard) and browsers (Chrome)
  use for a no-learning or private mode. "IncogBoard" builds on it, so it is easy to understand but weaker as a mark,
  and the store text should not suggest an affiliation with any browser's or keyboard's incognito mode.
- **Overall risk: low to medium** (no identical name found; descriptive root). **Before the Play submission:** search
  INCOGBOARD and INCOG in classes 9 and 42 on USPTO (Trademark Search), EUIPO (eSearch plus), WIPO Global Brand
  Database and IP India; consider a professional clearance search if the app is to be monetised or widely promoted.
- **Store wording:** "IncogBoard Keyboard" or "IncogBoard: private keyboard" in the title helps users and examiners;
  avoid "incognito mode" claims beyond what the app does (it has an incognito switch and auto-incognito apps).

## Kosha (old name, Wave 5-A, Part 3)

Date of the checks: 2026-11-23. Not legal advice. This is a first screen so the user can decide whether to keep the name. Nothing has been filed, registered or reserved.

## Summary

- **Overall risk: medium.** The name is a real Sanskrit and Hindi word with a cultural meaning, and it is already in use by several apps, a privacy product, a Sanskrit dictionary site, a jewellery brand and a BaaS company. None of these is an obvious identical mark in the input-method or keyboard class, but this screen could not query the official trademark registers directly, so the class 9 and class 42 picture is incomplete.
- **Recommendation:** keep "Kosha" only as "Kosha Keyboard" (or "Kosha keyboard") in every store listing and in the app name field where the store allows it, and **run a professional clearance search before the first public release**. If that search finds a live mark in class 9 or 42 that covers keyboards, input methods or privacy software, choose an alternative name.
- **Not checked from here:** the official search engines of USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO, IP India and UKIPO (they are JavaScript applications with no public API that this tool can call). Everything about them below comes from web search snippets, which show dates that may be old. Each status must be confirmed on the official site.

## Part D: common-word and cultural risk

- "Kosha" (कोश, কোশ) is a common Sanskrit, Hindi and Bengali word meaning a treasury, store, dictionary or sheath (the five *koshas* of Hindu philosophy). The Kosha brand explains it as "a treasury" on purpose.
- It is close to "kosher" in spelling but a different word. It is not a Jewish dietary term.
- Descriptive or cultural meanings weaken the claim of any single owner, but they do not remove conflict risk in the same classes. A mark that is descriptive of an Indic-language dictionary (SanKosha, Kosha.app) would still conflict with a later identical brand for software.

## Recommendation

1. **Keep the name** if the professional search comes back clear in classes 9 and 42. Use "Kosha Keyboard" everywhere the store and the launcher allow it, and add a line in the store listing saying what the app is. The brand (treasure chest) and the name keep the origin visible.
2. **Resolve the store conflict first.** "Kosha: Private Vault" is the closest market neighbour. Do not publish under a name that a Play Store reviewer could confuse with an existing privacy app, and check the Play Store search for "Kosha" by hand.
3. **Consider a backup name now,** while changes are cheap (app name in `strings.xml`, ARB files, store listings, the README). Good candidates must be checked in the same way. This document does not propose one.

## Steps for the user

1. Run the official searches listed in Part A on the USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO, IP India and UK IPO sites. Record each query, the date and the hit numbers. For every "KOSH" and "KOSHA" hit in classes 9, 38, 42 and 45 note the owner and status.
2. Search the Google Play store for "Kosha" and for "Kosha keyboard" in a browser with no account signed in.
3. Commission a clearance search from a trademark attorney in the countries where the app will be sold or promoted (at least the US, the EU, the UK and India). Give them the class list (9 and 42 for software, 38 and 45 for services) and this table. They can run the professional databases and give a written opinion. The attorney decides the search scope and the legal risk, not this document.
4. If a filing is wanted later, the user files themselves on the chosen office's site, after the attorney's advice. Nothing has been filed, and nothing in this repository files or reserves a name.
5. Do not register kosha domains or handles on the basis of this document until the name is decided. The domains above are already taken or resolving.
